# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Standards

Examples of constructive behavior include being respectful, accepting differing technical viewpoints, giving feedback on ideas rather than people, and taking responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, threats, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
