Assay & laboratory quality

ISO 11426:2021 covers cupellation (fire assay) for determination of gold on suitable samples, while ISO/IEC 17025:2017 sets international requirements for competent testing and calibration laboratories.

Fineness & terminology

ISO 9202:2026 is the current international standard specifying recommended ranges of fineness for precious-metal alloys used in jewellery. Country and market rules can still determine legal marking or stamping.

ISO 9202:2026 ↗

Industry terminology & responsible sourcing

CIBJO Blue Books provide industry nomenclature and guidance for precious metals and related jewellery sectors. RJC standards address responsible practices and chain of custody; these are useful references, not universal legal requirements.
